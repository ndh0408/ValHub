# ValVN product audit: analytics, store intelligence, Home, UX, sharing and notifications

Read-only audit of `D:\ValVN` at HEAD `009d1c1`, branch `claude/jolly-hawking-23o2j8`. I read the required docs and the code paths named in the brief, and left `.claude\worktrees` alone. I did not run `flutter analyze` or `flutter test`. `docs\PROGRESS.md` claims 0 analyze issues and 960 passing tests; I did not verify that. The repo status is unchanged from the start. Anything I could not prove by reading is marked **unverified**.

## 1. Executive summary

- **(A) Per-match analytics are solid; cross-match analytics are the gap.** Per-match stats (K/D/A, ACS, ADR, HS%, first bloods, KAST, MVP, result for every mode) are pure and unit-tested in `D:\ValVN\lib\core\domain\competitive\match_models.dart:1133-1301`. Across matches there is only a 10-match `RecentForm` plus an RR-only ledger. Agent, map, queue and attack/defense performance, and non-RR trends, do not exist. Clutches are not reliably derivable and must not be shown as a stat.
- **(A) One visible number is wrong today.** `RecentForm` mixes Deathmatch, where ACS is the total score, into win rate, K/D and mean ACS (PR-02).
- **(A) Several calculations live inside widgets** rather than pure tested functions (PR-15).
- **(B) The store shows price, VP, wallet, tier, owned, wishlist, chromas, levels and (in the sheet only) community votes and rating.** Radianite and upgrade costs are missing: `PvpApi.itemUpgrades` exists with zero callers.
- **(B) No store history is persisted.** Only the last storefront copy per account, an undated price map, the Night Market "seen" ids and the wishlist are stored. The UI shows no history, so nothing is fabricated. Because Riot has no past-store endpoint, every unrecorded day is lost (PR-03).
- **(C) Home matches `docs\design\HOME.md` closely.** It has 8 cards in IA order, pure snapshot builders, a startup gate, layout persistence and reset, consent gating and gated pollers. One misleading number ("đủ mua N skin", PR-04) and a few hygiene and spec deviations remain.
- **(D) The design system is consistent, with several debts.** Tablet, foldable and landscape run as stretched phone layouts. RTL and accessibility debt is growing, and guideline tests cover Home only. Orientation is not locked anywhere, which is good.
- **(E) There are no external deep links or share URLs.** Deep links exist only as local-notification payloads. Notification categories cover Daily Store, Wishlist and Night Market; Battle Pass, Rank, Community and LFG are absent. Community and LFG need push, which the privacy policy says does not exist.
- **Privacy.** RR history survives sign-out and "Xóa bộ nhớ đệm", includes rows for other players, has no delete control, and is not disclosed in the in-app policy (PR-08).
- **No generative AI code was found.** `google_mlkit_translation` (on-device machine translation of community posts) is AI-adjacent and needs an owner decision (PR-30).

Definitions used below:
- **Verdict tags (§2):** A = available and computed correctly. W = computed in a widget (violation). D = derivable but not computed. N = not reliably derivable, must not be shown.
- **Severity:** High = wrong data shown, or a time-critical gap against the owner's goals. Medium = policy, UX, accessibility or engineering gap affecting many users. Low = hygiene or polish.
- **Effort:** S is about a day, M is 2–5 days, L is more than a week.

## 5. What is already correct

- **Match model**
  - Parsing is defensive, UUIDs are lowercased, and `null` arrays and blank names are handled (`match_models.dart:19-23`, `MatchDetails.fromJson`).
  - DM, TDM, Escalation, custom, surrender and vote-draw handling is unit-tested (`match_models_test.dart`).
  - ADR excludes friendly damage and HS% uses hits with a "–" fallback.
  - Kills are rebuilt from per-round data when the top-level list is empty, as in 2026 responses.
- **Rank domain**
  - Tier tables are normalised across acts, and peak is computed per act.
  - "True peak" is labelled "trên thiết bị".
  - Daily RR groups at device-local midnight and the rank-up calculator follows SUMMARY §9.7; both are tested (`rank_calc_test.dart`).
  - The RR ledger dedupes, caps at 5,000 rows, serialises writes and degrades to memory. Backfill is limited to 5 pages.
- **Match details:** disk LRU cache, a "still processing" state for 404, batched name resolution and incognito respected.
- **Store**
  - The price chain (table, observed, P-5 behind a flag, reward label, tier fallback marked "≈") is pure.
  - Collection and wishlist value are pure and tested.
  - Night Market "seen" is local. The wishlist is per account with a pure hit finder.
  - The store share image excludes account IDs by default.
- **Home:** every item under "Done" in §3, each with tests. Those cover no overflow at 320×640 @200% up to 1366×1024, RTL, a hinge, reduced motion, `meetsGuideline` tap-target and contrast, and "hidden cards never call their API".
- **Notifications:** local only, inexact alarms, per-account ids cancelled at sign-out. The background check reads P-1 at most once per UTC day per account.
- **Platform and UI hygiene**
  - No orientation locks.
  - Every real `IconButton` has a tooltip.
  - Lists and grids are lazy.
  - `NetImage` sizes its decode to the layout, keeps a 30-day cache and evicts broken files.
  - `SkeletonShimmer` honours reduced motion.
  - `AccountChip` has a 48 dp hit target.
  - All sheet headers mark the title as a semantic header.
- **Dependencies:** no analytics or crash SDKs are present in `pubspec.yaml`.
