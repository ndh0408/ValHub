## Competitive research (2026-10-07)

Verified status: ValBuddy 2.2.2 (5 accounts, ads + remove-ads IAP), DailyVal 3.0.0 (removed remote pick/lock, check-in streak + ad-gated recovery, store history), Tracker 3.34.2 (session reports, team economy), Valking 3.2.3 (redesign), Spike Stats (last update May 2026), DAK.GG and Blitz active (Blitz exposed to Riot's 2026-10-06 game-memory block for unknown desktop apps). New: VALKI, TodayVal (home-screen widget), VALPRO ("data stays on your device"), ValoStore, VL.GG, and an iOS app "ValoHub" (name collision risk). Recurring complaints across them: login loops, slow history, ads/paywalls, wrong store data, account-safety worries. ValHub's answers this session: stay-signed-in fix, explainable free analytics (economy rule shown), honest saved-copy states, no remote lock, local store history.
