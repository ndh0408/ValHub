# Collaboration conduct

Use respectful, specific feedback focused on behavior, evidence and code.
Harassment, threats, impersonation and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable in project discussions or reviews.

Keep security reports and account data private; use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
For other conduct concerns contact the owner at the address published in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). The owner reviews reports and decides appropriate action;
this document does not promise a response deadline or an anonymous reporting
service. Repository access and contribution rights remain subject to LICENSE.
